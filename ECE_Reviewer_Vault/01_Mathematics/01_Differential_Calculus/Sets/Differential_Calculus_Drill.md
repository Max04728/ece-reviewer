### 1. Estimate $\sqrt[3]{27.3}$ using differentials.

**Given:** base x = 27; dx = 0.3

> [!success]- Answer
> **$\approx 3.0111$ (true value $3.01109\ldots$)**

> [!warning] Trap
> Computing f'(27) as (1/3)(27)^{2/3} with a positive exponent, giving a wildly wrong correction.

### 2. For $x = 2\cos t$, $y = 3\sin t$, find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ and $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}$.

**Given:** parametric ellipse

> [!success]- Answer
> **$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -\dfrac{3}{2}\cot t$, $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = -\dfrac{3}{4}\csc^3 t$**

> [!warning] Trap
> Dividing by dx/dt only once, or using d2y/dt2 = -3 sin t over d2x/dt2 = -2 cos t. Both are wrong.

### 3. Suppose $f(1) = 2$ and $f'(x) \le 5$ for all $x$. What is the largest possible value of $f(4)$?

**Given:** f(1) = 2; f' bounded by 5

> [!success]- Answer
> **$f(4) \le 17$**

> [!warning] Trap
> Multiplying the bound by 4 instead of by the interval length 3.

### 4. Show that $f(x) = |x|$ on $[-1,1]$ does not satisfy the conclusion of Rolle's theorem, and explain which hypothesis fails.

**Given:** absolute value function

> [!success]- Answer
> **Differentiability fails at $x=0$; the conclusion genuinely does not hold.**

> [!warning] Trap
> Claiming the theorem fails because f has a 'corner' without naming the failed hypothesis, or asserting the conclusion holds anyway.

<sub>from MATH-01-08</sub>

### 5. Find the value of $c$ guaranteed by the Mean Value Theorem for $f(x) = x^3 - x$ on $[0, 2]$.

**Given:** interval [0,2]

> [!success]- Answer
> **$c = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \approx 1.155$**

> [!warning] Trap
> Keeping the negative root, which lies outside the interval, or setting f'(x) equal to zero instead of the average slope.

<sub>from MATH-01-08</sub>

### 6. A function has $f''(x) = 6x(x-1)^2$. Find all inflection points.

**Given:** f'' in factored form; repeated root

> [!success]- Answer
> **Inflection at $x=0$ only; $x=1$ is not an inflection despite $f''(1)=0$.**

> [!warning] Trap
> Listing both roots of f''=0. An even-multiplicity root of f'' does not change sign, so it is not an inflection point.

<sub>from MATH-01-05</sub>

### 7. A rectangle has perimeter 40 m. Find the dimensions that maximise its area.

**Given:** P = 40 m

> [!success]- Answer
> **$10 \times 10$ m square, area $100$ m².**

> [!warning] Trap
> Introducing two variables and setting both partial derivatives - unnecessary, and the constraint must first reduce the problem to one variable.

<sub>from MATH-01-06</sub>

### 8. Prove that $f(x) = x^3 + 4x - 1$ has exactly one real root.

**Given:** cubic; existence and uniqueness

> [!success]- Answer
> **Exactly one real root, in $(0,1)$.**

> [!warning] Trap
> Proving existence with IVT and stopping. Uniqueness requires the Rolle contradiction argument.

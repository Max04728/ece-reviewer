### 1. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Tangent vs normal.** A question asking for the normal wants the negative reciprocal slope; reading past that word is a common, entirely avoidable loss.

<sub>from MATH-01-03</sub>

### 2. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Ignoring the physical domain.** A length cannot be negative; a radius must be positive. Check the optimum lies in the admissible range and check endpoints when the domain is closed.

<sub>from MATH-01-06</sub>

### 3. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Inflection needs a concavity change**, not merely $f''=0$. $f(x)=x^4$ has $f''(0)=0$ and no inflection.

<sub>from MATH-01-05</sub>

### 4. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Confusing $f'$ and $f''$ roles.** $f'$ locates extrema; $f''$ determines concavity and classifies extrema. A question about 'where the curve bends' is about $f''$ only.

<sub>from MATH-01-05</sub>

### 5. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Concluding the limit does not exist when L'Hopital fails.** If $f'/g'$ has no limit, no conclusion follows. Try $x + \sin x$ over $x$ as $x\to\infty$: the original limit is 1.

<sub>from MATH-01-01</sub>

### 6. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Relative error scales with the exponent.** $Q \propto x^n$ multiplies relative error by $n$: doubled for squares, halved for square roots, tripled for cubes.

<sub>from MATH-01-07</sub>

### 7. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Errors never cancel.** Even when the differential carries a minus sign, uncertainties add in magnitude. Absolute values are mandatory.

<sub>from MATH-01-07</sub>

### 8. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Rolle requires equal endpoint values.** Without $f(a)=f(b)$ you must use the MVT, not Rolle.

<sub>from MATH-01-08</sub>

### 9. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Stopping after one application.** Repeated application is legitimate while the form remains $0/0$ or $\infty/\infty$ — but stop the moment it is determinate, and use algebra (identities, factoring, dividing by the highest power) whenever it is faster.

<sub>from MATH-01-01</sub>

### 10. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Forgetting the endpoints** on a closed interval. Absolute extrema on $[a,b]$ require comparing $f$ at $a$ and $b$ as well.

<sub>from MATH-01-05</sub>

### 11. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Forgetting to multiply back by $y$** after logarithmic differentiation, and answering with $\frac{y'}{y}$ instead of $y'$.

<sub>from MATH-01-03</sub>

### 12. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Losing domain restrictions.** A rational function's critical points must lie in its domain; a point where the function is undefined is never an extremum even if $f'=0$ nearby.

<sub>from MATH-01-05</sub>

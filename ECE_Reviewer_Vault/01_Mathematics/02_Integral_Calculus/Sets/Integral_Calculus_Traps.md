### 1. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Leaving an $x$ behind.** A correct substitution eliminates every $x$ and every $dx$. A stray $x$ means the substitution is incomplete or inappropriate.

<sub>from MATH-02-02</sub>

### 2. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Confusing force with pressure.** Pressure is force per unit area; the hydrostatic integral yields force in newtons. Reporting a pressure when a force was asked is a missing step, not a rounding difference.

<sub>from MATH-02-15</sub>

### 3. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Confusing radius and height.** In $2\pi\int(\text{radius})(\text{height})$, the radius is the distance from the strip to the *axis of rotation*, and the height is the length of the strip. Swapping them gives a wrong answer that still looks plausible.

<sub>from MATH-02-12</sub>

### 4. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Wrong radius when the axis is translated.** Rotating about $y=k$ gives radius $|f(x)-k|$, not $|f(x)|$.

<sub>from MATH-02-11</sub>

### 5. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Radius errors in surface area.** About the $x$-axis the radius is $y$; about the $y$-axis it is $x$. The radius is the distance from the arc element to the axis.

<sub>from MATH-02-13</sub>

### 6. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Missing pole intersections.** Two polar curves can both pass through the pole at *different* angles, which $f(\theta)=g(\theta)$ never reveals. Check whether each curve reaches $r=0$.

<sub>from MATH-02-10</sub>

### 7. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Confusing average value with RMS.** They are different averages. Average value of a full sine cycle is 0; its RMS is $A/\sqrt{2}$. Power uses RMS.

<sub>from MATH-02-08</sub>

### 8. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Assuming a washer when the region touches the axis.** If the inner radius is zero the solid is a disk; treating it as a washer still works but adds unnecessary terms and invites errors.

<sub>from MATH-02-11</sub>

### 9. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Missing symmetry.** Checking even/odd before integrating over a symmetric interval can reduce the work to zero.

<sub>from MATH-02-03</sub>

### 10. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Sign errors from a region below the axis.** The moment $M_x$ will be negative, which correctly places $\bar{y}$ below the axis. Do not 'fix' the sign.

<sub>from MATH-02-14</sub>

### 11. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Forgetting to divide by $\ln a$** for $\int a^x dx$ with $a \neq e$.

<sub>from MATH-02-01</sub>

### 12. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **One divergent piece is enough.** For an interior discontinuity, both halves must converge. If either diverges, the whole integral diverges.

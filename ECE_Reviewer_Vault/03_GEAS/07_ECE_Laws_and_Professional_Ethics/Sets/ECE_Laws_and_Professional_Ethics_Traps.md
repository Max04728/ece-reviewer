# ECE Laws and Professional Ethics — Trap Recall

### 1. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Answering that any ECE may sign and seal plans.** Sec. 5(b) reserves the sole authority to provide consulting services and to sign and seal to the PECE. A registered ECE's Sec. 5(a) scope is broad but stops short of sealing, and sealing without that authority is punishable under Sec. 35.

### 2. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Quoting the critical-infrastructure penalty as prision mayor. Section 8 raises Section 4(a) acts against critical infrastructure to **reclusion temporal** with a fine of at least ₱500,000; prision mayor with a fine of at least ₱200,000 is the base row for 4(a) and 4(b).

<sub>from GEAS-07-07</sub>

### 3. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Answering “ECE” when asked which registrant may sign and seal electronics plans and documents: RA 9292 §5 reserves the seal, and consulting services, to the PECE. The correct answer must name the Professional Electronics Engineer.

<sub>from GEAS-07-02</sub>

### 4. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Applying the one-degree-higher rule to aiding, abetting or attempt. Section 5 penalties are **one degree lower** than the prescribed penalty for the offence, or a fine of ₱100,000 to ₱500,000, or both; the one-degree-higher rule belongs to §6 (RPC and special-law crimes through ICT) and §4(c)(2) (child pornography).

<sub>from GEAS-07-07</sub>

### 5. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Swapping the sensitive-data fine ceiling. Unauthorised processing of sensitive personal information is ₱500,000 to **₱4,000,000**, not ₱2,000,000; ₱2,000,000 is the personal-information ceiling under §25(a).

<sub>from GEAS-07-06</sub>

### 6. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Crediting the NTC's creation to RA 7925. The Commission was created by EO 546 in 1979; RA 7925 (1995) added responsibilities to an existing agency and named it principal administrator.

<sub>from GEAS-07-05</sub>

### 7. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Saying a Board decision is immediately final. Under RA 9292 §7 it is appealable to the Commission within fifteen (15) days from receipt, and becomes final and enforceable only if no appeal is taken.

<sub>from GEAS-07-03</sub>

### 8. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Attributing the 72-hour breach notification deadline to the text of RA 10173. Section 20(f) says **promptly** and sets the trigger (unauthorised acquisition plus a real risk of serious harm); the 72-hour deadline is imposed by the NPC's breach-notification circular issued under its rule-making power.

<sub>from GEAS-07-06</sub>

### 9. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Saying the CICC is chaired by the DOJ or the DICT Secretary. Section 25 chairs it with the **Executive Director of the ICTO-DOST**, with the NBI Director as Vice Chairperson.

<sub>from GEAS-07-07</sub>

### 10. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Answering “the NTC grants franchises.” RA 7925 §3(d) defines a franchise as a privilege conferred by Congress; the NTC grants the CPCN, and §16 makes both a franchise and a CPCN necessary before a public telecommunications entity may operate.

<sub>from GEAS-07-04</sub>

### 11. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> Quoting a passing average of 75% for the ECE board. RA 9292 §16 requires seventy percent (70%) **in each subject**, with a removal examination only for failed ratings of at least 60% when the majority of subjects were passed.

<sub>from GEAS-07-03</sub>

### 12. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Assuming a suspended or revoked registrant may keep signing.** Sec. 35(e) makes stamping or sealing after revocation or suspension an offence, and Sec. 24 requires a two-year wait before the Board may reinstate a revoked certificate.

# 03 — PRC and BEE Powers and Penal Provisions

> [!abstract] Scope
> Who regulates the ECE profession, what the Professional Regulation Commission and the Professional Regulatory Board of Electronics Engineering may each do, and the penalties for practising or sealing without authority.

## Core Concept

> [!tip] Intuition
> The Board writes and grades the examinations and hears the ethics cases; the Commission runs the licensure system, reviews what the Board issues, and is where the Board's decisions go on appeal.

**Two regulators, two statutes.** The Professional Regulation Commission is created by RA 8981 (PRC Modernization Act of 2000, approved 5 December 2000), which repealed PD 223. It is a three-man commission — one full-time Chairperson and two full-time Commissioners, appointed by the President for a term of seven (7) years **without reappointment** (RA 8981 §4) — attached to the Office of the President for general direction and coordination (§3). The Professional Regulatory Board of Electronics Engineering is created separately by RA 9292 §6 and sits **under the administrative control and supervision of the Commission**.

**Composition and tenure of the Board.** RA 9292 §6: a Chairman and two (2) members, appointed by the President from three (3) recommendees per position chosen and ranked by the Commission, which in turn chooses from five (5) nominees per position submitted by the accredited professional organization. Qualifications (§8): citizen and resident of the Philippines for at least five (5) consecutive years; good moral character and integrity; holder of a valid certificate of registration and PIC **as a PECE**; member in good standing of the APO; at least ten (10) years of active practice; no pecuniary interest, direct or indirect, in any school, academy, college, university or institution conferring a degree or certification required for admission to practice, nor in any review class, and not a member of the faculty or administration of such before taking the oath of office; and no conviction of an offence involving moral turpitude. Term of office (§9): three (3) years, **reappointment once** for another term; a vacancy is filled only for the unexpired portion, and continuous tenure may not exceed six (6) years.

**What the Board may do (RA 9292 §7).** Administer and implement the Act; administer oaths; adopt an official seal; issue, suspend or revoke certificates of registration and the corresponding PICs, or suspend a holder from practice, for justifiable cause after due process; maintain the roster of PECEs, ECEs and ECTs; issue, suspend or cancel special permits for foreign practitioners; prescribe and revise PECE licensing requirements; prepare, adopt and issue the syllabi of the ECE and ECT licensure subjects and frame the questions strictly within them; grant registration without examination subject to Commission review and approval; study curricula and facilities with CHED and TESDA and inspect schools; **adopt and administer a Code of Ethics and a Code of Technical Standards of Practice**; promulgate rules on the scope of practice and a CPE/CPD programme; prescribe minimum manning and manpower requirements in industrial plants and commercial establishments; formulate rules for electronics installations in buildings covered by the National Building Code, in coordination with the DPWH; and hear and decide violations of the Act, its IRR, the Code of Ethics and the Code of Technical Standards of Practice, with power to issue subpoena ad testificandum and subpoena duces tecum.

**The appeal route is the exam's favourite hinge.** RA 9292 §7: Board resolutions, orders and decisions in administrative cases may be appealed to the Commission within **fifteen (15) days** from receipt; the Commission may affirm, reverse, dismiss, deny the appeal or remand. If no appeal is taken within those 15 days the decision becomes final and immediately enforceable. RA 8981 §9(c) states the same rule for board decisions generally. Hearings that strictly concern the practice of the profession must be presided over by at least one (1) member of the Board, assisted by a Legal or Hearing Officer of the Commission; only the purely administrative cases may be delegated. Except in administrative cases, all Board resolutions embodying rules and regulations are subject to Commission review and approval.

**The Commission's own powers (RA 8981 §7).** Administer and enforce the regulatory policies for all professions; review, revise and approve board resolutions; administer and conduct the licensure examinations, fix their places and dates, approve results and release them, and impose suspension or prohibition from taking examinations on examinees who violate the rules; admit successful examinees to practice and issue the certificate of registration/professional licence and, at the option of the professional, the PIC; fix all fees; investigate **motu proprio** or on verified complaint any board member and, if found guilty, revoke or suspend his certificate and licence and recommend suspension or removal to the President; and — the unlicensed-practice hook — upon complaint under oath, investigate any person who practises a regulated profession without being registered and licensed, and if the evidence warrants, forward the records to the city or provincial prosecutor for the filing of the information.

**Penal provisions — know which law supplies the number.** Two penalties are routinely confused. (1) **RA 9292 §35** punishes nine enumerated acts by a fine of not less than ₱100,000 nor more than ₱1,000,000, **or** imprisonment of not less than six (6) months nor more than six (6) years, **or both**, in the discretion of the court: false or fraudulent statements to obtain a certificate or PIC; presenting or using another's certificate, PIC, APO membership card or seal, and allowing such use; presenting or using a revoked or suspended certificate; assuming, using, advertising or practising as PECE/ECE/ECT without being registered; stamping or sealing after revocation, suspension or removal from the roster; a PECE signing or sealing plans prepared by or under the supervision of another PECE without clearly indicating the part actually performed by the former; any person except the PECE or ECE in charge signing for electronics engineering work not actually performed by him; illegal wiretapping, cloning, hacking, cracking, piracy or other unauthorised electronic eavesdropping, or maintaining an unlicensed/unregistered communications system or device; and any violation of the Act, its rules, the Code of Ethics or the Code of Technical Standards of Practice. (2) **RA 8981 §15** punishes manipulation or rigging of examination results, secretly disclosing examination questions beforehand, or tampering with grades, by imprisonment of not less than six (6) years and one (1) day to not more than twelve (12) years, or a fine of ₱50,000 to ₱100,000, or both; if the offender is a Commission officer or employee or a board member, he is removed from office and suffers perpetual absolute disqualification from public office, in addition to those penalties. Accomplices get four (4) years and one (1) day to six (6) years or ₱20,000–₱49,000; accessories two (2) years and one (1) day to four (4) years or ₱5,000–₱19,000. Removal of a board member for cause is separately provided in RA 9292 §11 (President, upon recommendation of the Commission, after opportunity to be heard).

**Suspension, revocation, reinstatement, and the exam itself.** The Board, upon proper notice and hearing, may revoke or suspend a certificate and PIC, or cancel a special permit, for unprofessional or unethical conduct, malpractice, incompetence, violation of the Act, the IRR, the Code of Ethics or the Code of Technical Standards of Practice, or where fraud, deceit or false statement was used to obtain the certificate, PIC or permit (RA 9292 §23). Reinstatement may be granted **two (2) years after revocation**, on application and for sufficient reasons, if the applicant committed no illegal practice or violation during the revocation (RA 9292 §24). No certificate or PIC shall be issued to a person convicted by a competent court of a crime involving moral turpitude, of immoral or dishonourable conduct, or of unsound mind (§21). On the examination side: the ECE examination consists of written tests covering at least Mathematics, Applied Sciences, Engineering Economics, Laws and Ethics, Electronics, Communications, Computers, and Information and Communications Technology (§15); the ECT examination may be written and/or practical (§15); passing requires **70% in each subject**, with one removal examination allowed only where the candidate passed the majority of the subjects and the failed subject ratings were not lower than 60% (§16); and results must be released within fifteen (15) days after the examination (§17). The Board must submit results in all subjects, signed by its members, to the Commission within ten (10) days from the last day of examination unless extended for justifiable cause (RA 8981 §9(h)).

## Traps & Exam Notes

- Answering that a PRC Commissioner serves three years, or that the PRC Chairperson may be reappointed: RA 8981 §4 gives a seven (7) year term with no reappointment, while the three-year, one-reappointment term belongs to the RA 9292 §9 Board.
- Quoting a passing average of 75% for the ECE board. RA 9292 §16 requires seventy percent (70%) **in each subject**, with a removal examination only for failed ratings of at least 60% when the majority of subjects were passed.
- Saying a Board decision is immediately final. Under RA 9292 §7 it is appealable to the Commission within fifteen (15) days from receipt, and becomes final and enforceable only if no appeal is taken.
- Charging unlicensed practice under RA 8981 instead of RA 9292: the ₱100,000–₱1,000,000 fine or 6 months–6 years imprisonment is RA 9292 §35, while RA 8981 §15's 6 years and 1 day–12 years applies to rigging examinations, leaking questions or tampering with grades.
- Saying the PRC, not the Board, adopts the Code of Ethics and the Code of Technical Standards of Practice, or that the Board, not the PRC, issues the certificate of registration. RA 9292 §7 gives the Board the code; RA 8981 §7(e) makes the Commission issue the certificate and PIC.
- Answering that the Board may hear every administrative case by delegation. RA 9292 §7 requires that where the issue strictly concerns the practice of the profession, the hearing be presided over by at least one Board member, assisted by a Legal or Hearing Officer.
- Stating that any Board rule takes effect on promulgation. Except in administrative cases, Board resolutions embodying rules, regulations and policies are subject to review and approval by the Commission (RA 9292 §7).
- Naming three board members when the numbers are 3 recommended by the PRC from 5 APO nominees — reversing the 3 and the 5 is a standard distractor.

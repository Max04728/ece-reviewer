### 1. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Valuing $(F/A)$ at the wrong date.** $(F/A,i,n)$ lands on the date of the LAST payment, which earns no interest. Asking for the balance one period later needs one more factor of $(1+i)$; that is the same operation that turns an ordinary annuity into an annuity due.

### 2. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Dividing PW by n to get AW.** Straight division ignores interest entirely; the correct conversion uses (A/P,i,n), which always exceeds 1/n.

<sub>from GEAS-05-07</sub>

### 3. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Treating a positive rate of return as an accept decision.** The test is i* >= MARR; a project earning 10% against a 15% MARR must be rejected.

<sub>from GEAS-05-08</sub>

### 4. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Stopping the payback year count at the year that crosses.** Interpolate within that year: n = k + (unrecovered balance)/(next cash flow).

<sub>from GEAS-05-08</sub>

### 5. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Including sunk costs.** Money already spent does not change between alternatives and cannot affect the choice. Only future, differing cash flows belong in the analysis.

<sub>from GEAS-05-07</sub>

### 6. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Forgetting that salvage offsets each renewal.** The recurring outlay is FC - SV, not FC; the sinking-fund factor must be applied to the net amount.

<sub>from GEAS-05-06</sub>

### 7. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Depreciating the first cost instead of the basis.** The salvage value is never depreciated, so every method multiplies (FC - SV), not FC.

<sub>from GEAS-05-10</sub>

### 8. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Discounting benefits and costs at different rates.** Both sides must use the same social discount rate, or the ratio is meaningless.

<sub>from GEAS-05-09</sub>

### 9. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Adding the sub-period rates instead of compounding them.** $1.5\% \times 12 = 18\%$ is not the effective annual rate of 18% compounded monthly; $(1.015)^{12} - 1 = 19.56\%$ is.

<sub>from GEAS-05-02</sub>

### 10. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Flipping the interpolation weight.** The weight PW_1/(PW_1 - PW_2) belongs to the lower rate and to the present worth that is still positive.

<sub>from GEAS-05-08</sub>

### 11. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Discounting a time-0 flow.** The first cost at t = 0 is already a present amount; multiplying it by (1+i)^0 is harmless, but applying a (P/F) factor to it is not.

<sub>from GEAS-05-07</sub>

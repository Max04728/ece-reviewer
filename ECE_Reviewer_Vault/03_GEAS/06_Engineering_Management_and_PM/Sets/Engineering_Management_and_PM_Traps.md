### 1. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Calling a SWOT a TOWS.** No strategy exists until the quadrants are paired into SO/ST/WO/WT; a four-quadrant list with no pairings earns no strategy marks.

<sub>from GEAS-06-08</sub>

### 2. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Filling the canvas as nine unrelated lists.** The blocks must cohere — value proposition to segment, channels to relationship model, revenue streams against cost structure — or the model explains nothing.

<sub>from GEAS-06-08</sub>

### 3. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Using $LS - EF$ for total float.** Total float is $LS - ES$, equivalently $LF - EF$; mixing the two formulas gives $t + TF$ and misclassifies critical activities.

<sub>from GEAS-06-05</sub>

### 4. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Treating total float as free to use.** An activity with $TF = 2$ and $FF = 0$ shares its float with successors; consuming it silently removes their float even though the project date survives.

<sub>from GEAS-06-05</sub>

### 5. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Picking the longest path by counting activities.** The critical path is the largest sum of durations, not the path with the most activities; a two-activity path can beat a four-activity path.

<sub>from GEAS-06-05</sub>

### 6. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Classifying a competitor's price cut as a weakness.** It is an external threat; a weakness is something internal to the firm, such as a thin service network.

<sub>from GEAS-06-08</sub>

### 7. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Counting a signed term sheet as runway.** Until the funds are in the bank the burn continues unchanged, and the deadline for the next milestone does not move.

<sub>from GEAS-06-10</sub>

### 8. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Judging the paid engine by CAC alone.** What matters is LTV against CAC: CAC = P500 with LTV = P400 destroys value even though every acquisition 'converts'.

<sub>from GEAS-06-07</sub>

### 9. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Building a 'small version' of the full product as the MVP.** The MVP tests the riskiest assumption; every feature that is not part of that test is cost without learning.

<sub>from GEAS-06-07</sub>

### 10. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Calling every product change a pivot.** A pivot changes strategy while keeping the vision; changing a button label inside the same strategy is optimization.

<sub>from GEAS-06-07</sub>

### 11. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Shortening one of two parallel critical paths.** The other path still sets the duration; each period of real reduction costs the sum of the cheapest slopes on all parallel critical paths, not just the cheapest one overall.

<sub>from GEAS-06-06</sub>

### 12. What goes wrong here?

> [!success]- Trap
> **Using Cp to judge an off-center process.** Cp measures width only, so a process with Cp = 2.0 can still produce defects on one side; Cpk is the index that falls when the mean drifts.

<sub>from GEAS-06-02</sub>

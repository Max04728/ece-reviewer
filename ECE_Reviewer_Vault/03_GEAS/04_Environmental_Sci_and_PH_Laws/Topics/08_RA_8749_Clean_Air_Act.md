# 08 — RA 8749 Clean Air Act

> [!abstract] Scope
> Recall the Philippine Clean Air Act by number, year, administering agency, standard-setting structure, the airshed and non-attainment machinery, and the exact fines and management fund that an examiner asks about.

## Core Concept

> [!tip] Intuition
> RA 8749 works on two levels at once: it sets permissible concentrations in the air everyone breathes (ambient standards) and permissible concentrations at each pipe and tailpipe (emission standards), then hands the money collected from violations to a dedicated fund for air quality programmes.

**The statute and the agency.** Republic Act No. **8749**, the **Philippine Clean Air Act of 1999**, was signed on **23 June 1999** and took effect in 2000 after its implementing rules. It declares the policy of balancing development with environmental protection and recognises the 'polluters must pay' principle. **Section 34** makes the DENR the primary implementing agency and — the detail most often asked — **converts the Environmental Management Bureau from a staff bureau into a line bureau** for the purpose of enforcement. Section 12 requires the DENR to publish and periodically revise the national ambient air quality guideline values for criteria pollutants and to add hazardous air pollutants. Section 11 requires it to issue information on air pollution control techniques, including best available technology. The law creates the **Integrated Air Quality Improvement Framework** and the **Air Quality Control Action Plan** (§§7-8), and designates **airsheds** (§9) — areas of similar climate, meteorology and topology that share a common airshed governing board chaired by the **DENR Secretary**, with emissions trading permitted among sources within an airshed. Areas where a pollutant already exceeds the standard are designated **non-attainment areas** (§10), where new sources of the exceeded pollutant are barred without a corresponding reduction from existing sources.

**Ambient standards, emission standards, and the incineration ban.** Section 12 sets the **National Ambient Air Quality Guideline Values for criteria pollutants** on short-term and long-term averaging times: TSP 230 µg/Ncm (24 h) and 90 µg/Ncm (1 yr); PM10 150 µg/Ncm (24 h) and 60 µg/Ncm (1 yr); $\mathrm{SO_2}$ 180 µg/Ncm or 0.07 ppm (24 h) and 80 µg/Ncm or 0.03 ppm (1 yr); $\mathrm{NO_2}$ 150 µg/Ncm or 0.08 ppm (24 h) and 0.03 ppm (1 yr); CO 35 ppm (1 h) and 9 ppm (8 h); lead 1.5 µg/Ncm; and photochemical oxidants as $\mathrm{O_3}$ 140 µg/Ncm or 0.07 ppm (1 h). DAO 2016-08 adds PM2.5 at 25 µg/Ncm (24 h) and 15 µg/Ncm (annual). Emission standards for stationary sources are set under §19 and by subsequent DAO, with DENR-EMB permits to operate required; for mobile sources, §21 sets emission limits and §22 provides for the national inspection and maintenance programme and Certificates of Conformity for new vehicles. **Section 20 prohibits incineration of municipal, biomedical and hazardous waste** — the single most examinable prohibition in the Act — and it is a different prohibition from RA 9003 §48 (open burning of solid waste). **Section 29** bans leaded gasoline and engines requiring it, effective not later than eighteen months after enactment, which is why unleaded fuel is universal; §28 prohibits misfueling. **Section 30** requires the DENR to phase out **ozone-depleting substances** consistent with the **Montreal Protocol**, publishing the list within sixty days of enactment. Section 31 assigns PAGASA the monitoring of greenhouse gases with PAGASA coordinating with the DENR on the national climate plan, §32 requires a POPs inventory within two years, and §33 assigns radioactive emissions to the Philippine Nuclear Research Institute (PNRI).

## Traps & Exam Notes

- Quoting the Clean Air Act as RA 9003. RA 9003 is the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000; RA 8749 is the Philippine Clean Air Act of 1999.
- Saying the DENR-EMB is a staff bureau. RA 8749 §34 converted the EMB into a **line** bureau; that conversion is itself a favourite exam item.
- Attributing the incineration ban to RA 9003. The ban on incinerating municipal, biomedical and hazardous waste is RA 8749 **§20**; RA 9003 §48 bans open burning of solid waste.
- Calling the PAB the body that issues ECCs. ECCs come from the DENR-EMB under PD 1586; the PAB adjudicates air and water pollution violations and imposes fines.
- Quoting a per-day fine for a non-standard violation. The per-day structure (up to P100,000/day) is specifically for actual exceedance of standards by a **stationary source** under §45; §47 violations carry P10,000-P100,000 or imprisonment instead.
- Treating carbon dioxide as a criteria pollutant. Section 5(a) excludes $\mathrm{CO_2}$ from the definition of 'air pollutant'; the criteria pollutants are CO, $\mathrm{NO_2}$, $\mathrm{SO_2}$, $\mathrm{O_3}$, Pb and particulate matter.
- Confusing the Montreal Protocol with the climate treaties. RA 8749 §30 ties the ODS phase-out to Montreal (ozone), while the greenhouse-gas planning duty in §31 is tied to the UNFCCC.
- Assuming emission standards and ambient standards are the same numbers. Ambient standards govern the concentration in the air everyone breathes; emission standards govern the concentration at the source — a source can comply with its emission limit and still sit in a non-attainment area.

# ECT Reviewer — Start Here

Built for the **Electronics Technician Licensure Examination, Monday 19 October 2026**. One paper, one morning, no second chance.

## The paper

| Item | Fact |
| --- | --- |
| Date and time | **8:00 AM – 12:00 NN, one sitting (4 hours)** |
| Weight | **100%** — a single paper decides the licence |

## How to read this tonight

1. **Do not read front to back.** Read each file's **tables first** — the exam is table-recall, not prose-recall.
2. Work the order above. If time runs out, `05_Laws` and `06_Trap_Sheet` still get read: Laws is 10% of the paper for 30 minutes of reading, the best trade on the board.
3. Every file ends with a **Recall test** — answer aloud before opening the collapsed answers. Recognition is not recall, and the exam only pays for recall.
4. At the end, close everything and read only [[06_Trap_Sheet]].

## Pacing the paper

Four hours is generous only if you do not fight items. At 200 items:

| If the paper is | Seconds per item | Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 150 items | 96 s | Comfortable; read every option |
| 200 items | 72 s | Comfortable; skip and return on anything over 90 s |
| 250 items | 58 s | Two passes: answer all certainties first |

Rules that hold regardless of item count:

- **Answer everything.** There is no penalty for a wrong answer, so a blank is a guaranteed zero and a guess is a possible point.
- **Laws items are free points.** They are recall, not computation — never spend three minutes on a color-code item and then guess a statute.
- **Do not re-derive a formula twice.** Key it into the calculator once, write the number, move on.
- **Watch the unit in the answer choices.** Half the trap items are correct-number-wrong-prefix.
